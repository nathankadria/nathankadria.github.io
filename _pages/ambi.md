Dear Hiring Manager,

Over the years my **passion for robotics** has grown from being in **FLL and FIRST** teams when I was younger, to being obsessed with the complex automation systems use in modern automobile factories, to **designing automation systems from the ground up at <a href=‘https://www.nathankadria.com/projects/greensight/‘>Greensight</a> and <a href=‘https://www.nathankadria.com/projects/instron/‘>Instron</a>.** Besides my professional career, my education has also been focused on robotics, as I **designed a precise <a href=‘https://www.nathankadria.com/projects/capstone/‘>Wire EDM machine</a>** for my engineering capstone project. 
>With my experience leading the design and production of automation systems in fast-paced startups using Onshape,
>familiarity with rapid prototyping using 3D printing and sheet metal parts, and
>passion for everything robotics and automation,
>I’m confident I would be a great fit as a **Mechanical Engineer at Ambi Robotics.**

In my co-op experiences at fast-paced startups, **I took ownership of projects while collaborating across teams.** Most recently, I led the mechanical design of <a href=‘https://www.nathankadria.com/projects/greensight/‘>WeatherHive</a>, an autonomous drone silo system capable of storing, charging, launching, and loading 10 weather drones, and led the production of 6 of these systems, all in just a few months. **I used Onshape to design 3D printed and sheet metal parts** to fill in the gaps that the off the shelf parts I specced couldn’t. I avoided complex machined parts whenever possible, but didn’t hesitate to take matters into my own hands and machine/fabricate a part myself when the need arose. Throughout the design and production process **I coordinated constantly with other engineering teams,** making dummy systems for them to test with to allow for parallel development and seamless integration down the line.
>I expect the process of designing, testing, and iterating on automation systems at Ambi closely resembles the development cycle I followed at Greensight with WeatherHive.

I got **hands-on experience with large scale automation systems at Instron** as a Mechanical Automation Engineer co-op. In this role I added retrofits to and expanded documentation for existing automation systems, and <a href=‘https://www.nathankadria.com/projects/instron/‘>designed an automation system</a> for a customer quote. **This system was designed to cooperate with human operators** to test hundreds of vials of different form factors.
>I used a vision system to identify failures, 
>specced pneumatic actuators to reduce control complexity, 
>designed a removable vial rack for easy human-machine interaction, and 
>built the system around off-the-shelf and existing Instron parts to reduce development time.

I am excited by the possibility of **working with Ambi to push the boundaries of robotics and automation.** Please feel free to learn more <a href=‘https://www.nathankadria.com/‘>about me</a> or contact me at **nathankadria@gmail.com / (774) 303-0696.** I look forward to the possibility of discussing how my skills, experience, and passions align with Ambi’s mission.

Thank you for your time and consideration.
