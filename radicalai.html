Dear Hiring Manager,

Over the years my passion for robotics has grown from being in FLL and FIRST teams when I was younger, to designing automation systems from the ground up at Greensight and Instron. Besides my professional career, my education has also been focused on robotics, as I designed a precise Wire EDM machine for my engineering capstone project. 

With my experience leading the design and production of automation systems at industry-leading companies and fast-paced startups, and passion for everything robotics and automation, I’m confident I would be a great fit as a Mechanical Engineer at Radical AI.

In my co-op experiences at fast-paced startups, I took ownership of projects while collaborating across teams. Most recently, I led the mechanical design of WeatherHive, an autonomous drone silo system capable of storing, charging, launching, and loading 10 weather drones, and led the production of 6 of these systems, all in just a few months. I designed 3D printed and sheet metal parts to fill in the gaps that the off the shelf parts I specced couldn’t. I avoided complex machined parts whenever possible, but didn’t hesitate to take matters into my own hands and machine/fabricate a part myself when the need arose. Throughout the design and production process I coordinated constantly with other engineering teams, making dummy systems for them to test with to allow for parallel development and seamless integration down the line.

I got hands-on experience with large scale automation systems at Instron as a Mechanical Automation Engineer co-op. In this role I added retrofits to and expanded documentation for existing automation systems, and designed an automation system for a customer quote. This system was designed to cooperate with human operators to test hundreds of vials of different form factors.

I used a vision system to identify failures,  specced pneumatic actuators to reduce control complexity,  designed a removable vial rack for easy human-machine interaction, and  built the system around off-the-shelf and existing Instron parts to reduce development time.

I am excited by the possibility of working with Radical AI to push the boundaries of automation and robotics. Please feel free to learn more about me or contact me at nathankadria@gmail.com / (774) 303-0696. I look forward to the possibility of discussing how my skills, experience, and passions align with Radical’s mission.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Best,

Nathan Kadria
