Atomic Semi

Dear Hiring Manager,

Over the years my passion for manufacturing has grown from drooling over newly-attainable FDM 3D printers when I was in high school, to becoming obsessed with all kinds of conventional machining, to designing and building a novel format of wire EDM machine to cut DED printed parts off of build plates. I’m constantly learning everything I can about manufacturing. I dream of one day owning a shop filled with every kind of machine I can imagine, or working somewhere that lets me use theirs!

With my experience leading the design and production of automation systems in fast-paced startups, mastery of the iterative design process using 3D printed and sheet metal parts, and passion for all kinds of manufacturing, I’m confident I would be a great fit at Atomic Semi.

During my time at fast-paced startups, I took ownership of projects while collaborating across teams. Most recently, I led the mechanical design of WeatherHive, an autonomous drone silo system capable of storing, charging, launching, and loading 10 weather drones, and led the production of 6 of these systems, all in just a few months. I used designed 3D printed, sheet metal, and machined parts and carefully selected COTS parts from vendors. I avoided complexity and machined parts wherever possible, but didn’t hesitate to take matters into my own hands and machine/fabricate a part myself when necessary. Throughout the design and production process I coordinated constantly with other engineering teams, making dummy systems for them to test with to allow for parallel development and seamless integration down the line. I conducted subsystem testing and validation before scaling up production, and iterated on designs to ensure the Hive was reliable and effective. WeatherHive met the requirements for the initial customer, and is a successful product that continues to be marketed to new customers.

I expect the process of designing, testing, and iterating on systems at Atomic Semi closely resembles the development cycle I followed at Greensight with WeatherHive.

My engineering capstone project also gave me experience working with novel manufacturing processes. In order to have any chance of breaking into a market with well established players like Mitsubishi, FANUC, and Makino, we had to address a niche in the market at a fraction of the cost. My group designed a machine to cut metal 3D printed parts (DED) off of build plates for an aerospace client.

Within a few months, my group designed, built, and delivered a WEDM machine that satisfied the unique requirements of our client.

I am excited by the possibility of working with Atomic Semi to push the boundaries of semiconductor manufacturing. Please feel free to learn more about me or contact me at nathankadria@gmail.com / (774) 303-0696. I look forward to the possibility of discussing how my skills, experience, and passions align with Atomic’s mission.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Best, Nathan Kadria
